What does R² (0.9053) mean?

R² indicates how much of the variation in house prices is explained by the model on the evaluation data. Specifically, 90.5% of the variance in property values across Indian metropolitan corridors is accounted for by square footage, rooms, location, and structural amenities.

What does MAE mean?

Mean Absolute Error measures the typical absolute discrepancy between actual transaction rates and model predictions. On average, the model's estimate deviates by approximately {{ metrics.metrics.mae|inr }} on unseen properties.

What does RMSE mean?

Root Mean Squared Error penalizes larger prediction deviations more heavily than smaller ones. Because high-value penthouses have higher absolute dollar swings, RMSE provides a rigorous risk ceiling for financial budgeting.
